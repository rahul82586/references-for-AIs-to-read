# Two Factor Authentication (2FA)

Overview
2-Factor Authentication (2FA) is a security method that adds an extra layer of protection to accounts. It requires users to provide a second
form of identification, like a mobile-generated code, in addition to their password. This dual authentication enhances security and helps
prevent unauthorized access as it requires both something known (password) and something possessed (mobile device) for account
verification.
Before You Begin
1. Authenticator app on your mobile.
Enabling 2FA: Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Access your bridge account
2. Navigate to your profile
3. Click on Two-factor authentication


4. Open the authenticator app on your mobile device
5. Scan the code displayed on the bridge using the app
6. After scanning, you will promptly receive a code on your Authenticator app.
7. Input your current password and the received code
8. Confirm by selecting the submit button
Voila! We are done. Now, your profile is secured with Two-Factor Authentication.


Disabling the Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
To disable the 2FA
1. Log in to your bridge account.
2. Navigate to your profile.
3. Click on the "Two-factor authentication" option.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Select the "Disable Two-factor authentication" option.

Reset the Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
To reset the 2FA
1. Log in to your bridge account.
2. Click on "Reset two-factor authenticator.”


3. Enter the code sent to your email.
You should now be able to log in to the bridge. If you wish to enhance account security, please set up the new Two-Factor Authentication
(2FA) by following the same steps mentioned above.
