# Workspace

Overview
Workspaces are virtual environments that provide users with a personalized and organized area to manage and interact with content.
Users can customize their Workspaces by arranging relevant modules and components to streamline their workflow. This feature
enhances efficiency by allowing users to centralize resources and tailor their workspace to suit individual preferences and tasks.
Creating a Workspace
To create a Workspace
1. Locate Workspace and then click the “Add” button
2. Fill out the required field “Workspace Name”
3. Click “Create” to create the workspace
Arranging your Workspace
To arrange your Workspace
1. Click “Add Screen” and select the components that you want to display in your workspace.


Notes: Workspace can allow up to 4 screens or components
Deleting a Workspace
To delete a Workspace
1. Click on the “Delete” option available
2. Click the “Delete” button to confirm the deletion.
