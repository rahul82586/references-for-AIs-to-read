# How To Restart Centroid Bridge

Overview
This page will help you restart your Centroid Bridge.
You can follow the Step-by-step process below to restart your Centroid Bridge.
1. Go to your Account which can be located on the lower left part of your bridge:
2. Click on your Account and it will open the Account Settings and you should be able to see the Restart button.
3. Click on the Restart Button and enter the reason why you want to Restart your Centroid Bridge.


Impact of the Bridge Restart
This will result in a brief downtime which will lead to the disconnection of all the MT Gateway/Feeder and FIX connections
Downtime will also result in a price halt and trade rejection
Schedule of the Bridge Restart
We highly suggest scheduling the bridge restart during the following windows:
EOD or FX Market Rollover
Weekend
Whenever you find it convenient for your operation
Actions that require to perform a Bridge Restart
Adding a new Maker
Adding a new Taker
Enabling a new Taker
Symbol Volume Digits Changes
Various Config changes
Native system configuration (done by the support team)
Maker Connection config changes
Taker Connection config changes

It is also recommended to perform post-checks after a Centroid Bridge Restart, to make sure everything is connected and working
as expected.
